# RCA Pricing Model & Service Tiers
**Strategic Tiers for NIST 800-171 & CMMC Compliance**

## Overview
Reetz Cyber Automation (RCA) offers three engagement tiers designed to scale with the complexity of the client's OT environment and the urgency of their CMMC certification timeline. We utilize the scalable Syrinx Architecture, allowing us to selectively deploy sections of our AI flock as your cybersecurity maturity grows.

---

## Tier 1: Strategic Audit (Portable Rig)
*Best for: One-time gap analysis and CMMC baseline assessments.*

- **Scope**: Multi-subnet scan utilizing the portable RCA Audit Rig. Deployment of **The Gaggle** (for non-intrusive asset inventory) and **The Flyway** (for high-level NIST 800-171 Rev 3 mapping).
- **Deliverables**: Gap Analysis Report (PDF), Prioritized Remediation Roadmap, CMMC Maturity Scorecard.
- **Service Type**: One-time implementation.
- **Target Price**: **$6,500 - $9,500** (site-based).

---

## Tier 2: Gaggle-Monitor (Hardware Lite)
*Best for: Continuous compliance visibility, threat hunting, and asset inventory without autonomous response.*

- **Scope**: Permanent deployment of the RCA "Lite" Hardware Appliance (Fanless/Ruggedized). Includes the continuous deployment of our Intelligence agents—**The Quill**—layering advanced anomaly detection and ReAct-based root cause analysis over the baseline.
- **Deliverables**: Hardened Appliance, Local Dashboard Access, Monthly Compliance Drift Reports, Active Threat Intelligence.
- **Service Type**: Annual Syrinx License + Hardware Cost.
- **Target Price**: 
    - **One-time Hardware/Setup**: $4,500
    - **Annual Syrinx License**: **$18,000 - $24,000/yr**

---

## Tier 3: Branta-SOC (Hardware Pro)
*Best for: Full autonomous defense, dark network isolation, and sub-hour mitigation requirements.*

- **Scope**: Flagship RCA "Pro" Hardware Appliance (High-compute for local LLM inference). Unlocks the full **24-Agent Branta Flock**, engaging **The Wedge** for precision-targeted automated containment and the full **Branta** core for orchestration. 
- **Deliverables**: High-Performance Appliance, Full Autonomous SOC Access, Sub-Second Mitigation, Priority CMMC Audit Evidence Packages.
- **Service Type**: Premium Annual Branta License + Pro Hardware.
- **Target Price**:
    - **One-time Hardware/Setup**: $12,000+
    - **Annual Branta License**: **$45,000 - $75,000/yr**

---

## Variable Factors
Prices may be adjusted based on the following:
1. **Node Count**: Significant discounts available for sites with >500 IP-connected industrial devices.
2. **OT Protocol Complexity**: Multi-vendor sites (e.g., mixing Rockwell, Siemens, and Schneider) may require additional setup time.
3. **Multi-Site Discounts**: 15% discount for 3+ sites under a single MSA.

*Prices are directional and subject to final site survey and SOW approval.*
