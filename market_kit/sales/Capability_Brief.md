## The Challenge: CMMC Deadline Urgency
Defense Industrial Base (DIB) contractors face a critical mandate to enforce NIST 800-171 controls under CMMC 2.0. Traditional security tools fail to address the complexity of **Operational Technology (OT)** environments, leading to compliance gaps, audit friction, and unmitigated risk.

## The Solution: The SENTINEL-RCA "Digital Hive Mind"
RCA provides a "Living Compliance" ecosystem powered by a **24-agent Digital Hive Mind**. Unlike standard SOAR tools, our SOC employs autonomous LLM-driven specialized agents that collaborate to detect, investigate, and remediate threats in real-time.

### Core Technology: The Engine
- **Passive & Active Discovery**: Non-intrusive asset identification via `SENTINEL-SCOUT`.
- **Industrial Probing**: Native support for Modbus, EtherNet/IP, and OT protocols.
- **Automated Mapping**: Real-time alignment with NIST 800-171 Rev 2/3 and CMMC 2.0.
- **Stateful Investigation**: Full case lifecycle management via `SENTINEL-MANAGER` with **SQLite WAL** persistence.

### The Agentic Advantage (The 4-Pillar Framework)
Our SOC is a fleet of specialized AI agents working in tandem:

1. **Surveillance (Operations)**: `SENTINEL-LOG-GUARDIAN` normalizes logs via NLP, while `SENTINEL-TRAFFIC-SIEVE` identifies exfiltration attempts.
2. **Cognitive Investigation (Intelligence)**: `SENTINEL-INVESTIGATOR` conducts LLM-driven root cause analysis (ReAct reasoning), supported by `SENTINEL-LIBRARIAN` (Institutional RAG Memory).
3. **Active Defense (Action)**: `SENTINEL-RESPONDER` executes isolation with a dead-man's switch, while `SENTINEL-PATCHPILOT` drafts idempotent fix scripts.
4. **Governance (Business)**: `SENTINEL-AUDITOR` generates submission-ready reports, mapped directly to NIST controls.
