# Sentinel-RCA — Capability Brief
**Autonomous Multi-Agent SOC for NIST 800-171 & CMMC 2.0 Compliance**

## The Challenge: CMMC Deadline Urgency
Defense Industrial Base (DIB) contractors face a critical mandate to enforce NIST 800-171 controls under CMMC 2.0. Traditional security tools fail to address the complexity of **Operational Technology (OT)** environments, leading to compliance gaps, audit friction, and unmitigated risk.

## The Solution: The RCA "SOC-in-a-Box"
RCA provides a "Zero-Phone-Home" security ecosystem powered by a **24-agent Digital Hive Mind**. Deployed as a self-contained, air-gapped hardware appliance, our SOC employs autonomous LLM-driven specialized agents that operate strictly on-premise for absolute data sovereignty.

### Core Technology: The Engine
- **Zero-Impact Discovery**: Passive & active non-intrusive asset identification via `SENTINEL-SCOUT`.
- **Industrial Probing**: Protocol-agnostic support for Modbus, EtherNet/IP, and PROFINET (80% market coverage).
- **Automated Mapping**: Real-time alignment with NIST 800-171 Rev 2/3 and CMMC 2.0.
- **Stateful Investigation**: Full case lifecycle management via `SENTINEL-MANAGER` with **SQLite WAL** persistence.

### The Agentic Advantage (The 4-Pillar Framework)
Our SOC is a fleet of specialized AI agents working in tandem:

1. **Surveillance & Discovery (Operations)**: `SENTINEL-SCOUT` maintains real-time asset inventory, while `SENTINEL-TRAFFIC-SIEVE` identifies exfiltration attempts via non-intrusive metadata analysis.
2. **Cognitive Investigation (Intelligence)**: `SENTINEL-TRIAGE` classifies alerts using deep-packet inspection (DPI), while `SENTINEL-INVESTIGATOR` conducts on-premise root cause analysis (LLM-driven ReAct reasoning).
3. **Active Defense (Action)**: `SENTINEL-RESPONDER` executes secure isolation with a dead-man's switch, while `SENTINEL-PATCHPILOT` drafts idempotent remediation scripts for human approval.
4. **Governance & Compliance (Business)**: `SENTINEL-GOVERNOR` serves as the consolidated compliance authority, auto-mapping findings to NIST controls and auto-tuning security rules.
