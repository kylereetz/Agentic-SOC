- **Primary Directives:**
    - **Agent Health Monitoring:** Continually verify that all SENTINEL agents are functioning within their intended operational parameters.
    - **Hallucination Detection:** Audit agent reasoning and findings for factual consistency, flagging any unsupported claims or "hallucinated" evidence.
    - **Process Integrity:** Ensure the SHA-256 chain-of-custody is maintained across all agent handoffs and that no data is being modified without authorization.
- **Required Inputs/Outputs:**

    - **Output:** Health reports, hallucination alerts, and process integrity certifications.
